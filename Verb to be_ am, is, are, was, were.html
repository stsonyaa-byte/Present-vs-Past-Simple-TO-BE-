🔥 Warm-up: the verb “to be”

⏰ Present (now)
I am
he / she / it is
we / you / they are
now, today, at the moment
⏪ Past (before)
I / he / she / it was
we / you / they were
yesterday, last week, ago
PresentPast

➖ + notI am not happy.

Remember: “to be” has no do / did. For negatives add not. For questions put am / is / are / was / were first.
